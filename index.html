About

Born and raised in LA. I make pictures that sell the product and tell a story at the same time.

I studied Multimedia Production at Cal State Northridge and have spent the past decade working across product, fashion, and marketing photography. Today, I lead product photography at GOAT, shaping imagery at the intersection of footwear, fashion, and culture.

Outside of that work, I take on select freelance projects spanning campaigns, lookbooks, and portraiture. I approach each project from concept through final image, bringing together photography and art direction to create work that feels considered, distinct, and visually driven.
